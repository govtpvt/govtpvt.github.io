Merit List, Waiting List and Reserve List Explained

A recruitment result can include several lists. Each one tells you something different about where you stand.

ListWhat it means
Qualified listCandidates who cleared the cut-off for the next stage
Provisional merit listCandidates selected subject to documents and medical
Final selection listCandidates offered appointment
Waiting or reserve listCandidates who may be offered posts that stay vacant

Waiting lists in practice

Vacancies open when selected candidates decline, fail medical or are found ineligible. The board then issues a supplementary list. Validity is usually limited, often to a year.

All of these lists appear among the Sarkari results on Govtpvt. Related: probation and joining.
