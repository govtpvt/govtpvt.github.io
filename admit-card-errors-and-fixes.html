Admit Card Errors and How to Get Them Fixed

Admit cards are generated from your application data, so most errors trace back to the form. Some errors are cosmetic. Others can stop you at the gate.

ErrorRiskWhat to do
Minor spelling difference in nameLow if photo ID matchesCarry an ID with the same spelling; email the helpline
Wrong or missing photoHighContact the board at once; carry extra photos and the application printout
Wrong categoryMedium, matters at result stageWrite to the board with your certificate
Centre far from chosen cityNot an error in most examsPlan travel; change requests are rarely allowed
Wrong date of birthHigh at document verificationRequest correction in writing and keep the reply

How to write to the helpline

Give your registration number, exam name, the exact error and a scan of the admit card and your ID. Ask for written confirmation.

Download links for upcoming exams are listed on the admit cards and hall tickets page. See also the correction window guide.
