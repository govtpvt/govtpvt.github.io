Government Exam Checklist

From notification to joining: what to do at each stage, and where to check it. Prepared by Govtpvt, India's government and private jobs portal.

1. Before you apply

  • Read the official notification PDF, not only a summary. Find new openings under latest government job notifications.
  • Check the crucial date for age and qualification, and the relaxation table for your category.
  • Confirm post-wise eligibility and the vacancies in your own category.
  • Note four dates: opening, closing, fee payment and correction window.

2. While applying

  • Apply only on the portal named in the notification.
  • Type your name and date of birth exactly as on your class 10 certificate.
  • Preview the form before paying, then save the final form and the payment receipt as PDF.

3. Admit card

  • Download it the day it is released. Track releases under admit cards and hall tickets.
  • Check name, photo, category, centre, shift and reporting time.
  • Carry the original ID named on the card and two printed copies.

4. After the exam

  • Download your response sheet and the provisional key from answer keys and objection notices.
  • Calculate your raw score with the marking scheme, including negative marking.
  • Raise objections only with a standard reference, and within the window.

5. Result

  • Check the qualified list, cut-off and scorecard under Sarkari results and merit lists.
  • Read the next-stage notice the same day: skill test, physical test, interview or document verification.

6. Document verification and joining

  • Originals plus two sets of self-attested copies: marksheets, degree, category or EWS certificate in the required format, ID and photographs.
  • A no-objection certificate if you are already employed in government.
  • Keep every admit card and scorecard until you have the appointment letter.
For entrance exams: counselling registration is separate from the exam. Follow admission and counselling updates from the day the result is declared.
Keep a fallback: a job alongside preparation reduces pressure. See private jobs for freshers and experienced candidates.
Safety: no recruiting authority asks for money by phone, messaging app or personal bank transfer. Fees are paid only on the official portal.
