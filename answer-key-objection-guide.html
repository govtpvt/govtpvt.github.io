How to File an Answer Key Objection

Most recruitment bodies publish a provisional answer key and let candidates challenge it for a few days. A well-filed objection takes ten minutes.

Steps

  1. Download the provisional key and your response sheet.
  2. Mark questions where you believe the key is wrong.
  3. Find a reliable source for each: a standard textbook, an official report or an act.
  4. Log in to the objection portal before the deadline and select the question ID.
  5. Enter the answer you claim is correct and upload or cite the source.
  6. Pay the fee per question if one applies, and save the receipt.

What gets accepted

Factual errors with clear sources, questions with two correct options, and questions with no correct option. Disagreements over interpretation rarely succeed.
