State Government Job or Central Government Job: What Actually Differs

Both are government service, but the two differ in ways that affect where you live and how your career moves.

PointCentralState
Recruiting bodiesUPSC, SSC, RRBs, IBPS and othersState PSC, staff selection or subordinate boards
PostingAnywhere in India, often with transfersWithin the state
Pay structureCentral pay commission levelsState pay rules, often modelled on the centre but revised on a different schedule
LanguageEnglish and Hindi; regional languages in several examsState language is often compulsory
ReservationCentral lists; open to all statesState lists; benefits usually limited to residents
CompetitionNationalMostly within the state

How to choose

  • If staying near home matters most, state services and state-wise vacancies in central exams are the realistic options.
  • If you are comfortable moving, central exams open far more posts.
  • If you belong to a reserved category, check whether your community is on the central list as well as the state list.

Preparing for both

The general studies and aptitude portions overlap heavily. The state paper and language are the extra work. See recruitment bodies in India for who conducts what.
