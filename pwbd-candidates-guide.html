Government Exams for Candidates With Disabilities: Scribes, Extra Time and Reservation

Persons with benchmark disabilities (PwBD) have statutory reservation in government recruitment, along with facilities during the exam. Using them correctly needs some paperwork in advance.

Reservation and identified posts

A share of vacancies is reserved across categories of disability, but only in posts identified as suitable for that category. The vacancy table shows which posts are open to which category; check it before applying.

Certificate

A disability certificate from a competent medical authority, or a UDID card, showing a disability of at least the benchmark percentage. Carry the original to every stage.

Scribe and compensatory time

  • Eligible candidates may use a scribe, their own or one from the board's panel, as the notification allows.
  • The scribe's qualification must normally be lower than the candidate's.
  • Compensatory time is given per hour of the exam.
  • The request and declaration forms are annexed to the notification and must be submitted on time.

Age and fee

Age relaxation is in addition to any category relaxation, and the application fee is waived in most central recruitments.

At the centre

Reach early; verification of scribe documents takes time. Ground-floor seating can be requested in advance.
