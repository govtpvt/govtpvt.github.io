Who Recruits for What: A Map of Government Recruitment Bodies in India

Government recruitment in India is not run by one office. Dozens of bodies advertise separately, each on its own website and calendar. This page maps the main ones so you know where a given kind of post comes from.

Entrance exams are separate

Bodies such as the National Testing Agency conduct entrance and eligibility tests for admission and fellowships. These lead to seats and eligibility, not directly to jobs. Their counselling schedules are tracked under admissions and counselling updates.

How to use this map

  1. Pick the two or three bodies whose posts match your qualification and age.
  2. Learn their usual calendar; most repeat every year.
  3. Check one consolidated list weekly instead of every website daily. All of the above appear in the latest government job notifications.
  4. After applying, follow the same body through admit cards, answer keys and results.

Private sector openings follow no such structure; they are listed separately under private jobs.
