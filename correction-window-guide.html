The Correction Window: What You Can Change After Submitting a Form

After the last date to apply, many recruiting bodies open a short correction window. It is the only sanctioned way to fix a submitted form, and it comes with limits.

What is usually editable

  • Spelling of your name, and parents' names
  • Date of birth, gender and category, sometimes only once
  • Photograph and signature
  • Educational details
  • Exam city preferences

What usually stays locked

  • Registered mobile number and email
  • The post applied for, in some recruitments
  • Anything after the window closes

Costs and limits

Some bodies charge a correction fee, and a higher one for a second correction. A change of category may require you to pay the difference in application fee; a refund is not given when the change lowers the fee.

How to use it well

  1. Note the window dates on the day you apply.
  2. Compare the submitted form line by line with your certificates.
  3. Make all changes in one sitting and download the revised form.

If there is no window

Write to the body with documentary proof before the admit card is issued. Do not submit a second application to fix the first; duplicates are often cancelled together.

Correction notices are published separately. Watch the notifications list on Govtpvt for them.
