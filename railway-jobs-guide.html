Railway Jobs in India: A Beginner Guide

Indian Railways is one of the largest employers in the country. Recruitment is split across several boards and categories.

Main categories

  • Non-technical popular categories: clerks, station masters, commercial staff
  • Technical: junior engineers, technicians
  • Level 1 posts: track maintainers, helpers and similar roles
  • Loco pilots and paramedical staff
  • Protection force constables and sub-inspectors

Stages

Computer based tests, followed by skill, aptitude or physical tests depending on the post, then document verification and medical.

Following notices

Each board publishes the same central notice, and candidates apply to one board. New railway notices appear on the latest government jobs page of govtpvt.com. Related: computer based test guide.
