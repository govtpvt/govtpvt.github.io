Contract, Deputation or Regular: Reading the Nature of a Government Vacancy

Not every government vacancy is a permanent job. The notification states the nature of the engagement, usually in one line that is easy to skip.

TypeWhat it meansLook for
Regular or direct recruitmentAppointment to a sanctioned post with probation, confirmation and service benefitsA pay level or pay scale
ContractualEngagement for a fixed term, renewable at the employer's discretionConsolidated monthly remuneration and a contract period
DeputationOpen only to serving government employees who move on loanThe words deputation or absorption, and a required pay level
Apprentice or traineeTraining with a stipend; no promise of a jobReference to the Apprentices Act or a stipend
Tenure or project postTied to a project's durationThe phrase co-terminus with the project

Why it matters

  • Contract service usually does not count for pension or seniority.
  • Renewal is not a right, however long you have served.
  • Age limits and reservation rules can differ for contractual posts.

How to decide

A contract post in a good organisation can be worth taking for the experience, provided you go in knowing its end date. Keep applying for regular posts alongside.
